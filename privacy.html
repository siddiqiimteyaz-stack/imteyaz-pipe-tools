Privacy Policy — IMTEYAZ SKILL HUB

Last updated: October 2026

IMTEYAZ SKILL HUB ("the App") is a pipe & structural fabrication training and calculator app, developed and maintained by an individual developer. This page explains what data the App does and does not collect.

What we collect

The developer does not collect, receive, or store any personal data. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

What is saved on your device

To make the App convenient, it saves a small amount of data only on your own device (in your browser's local storage):

  • the numbers you last typed into the calculators, so they are there when you return;
  • display choices, such as Sunlight mode and the columns you picked in the schedule chart;
  • your reading position in the course book and other lesson progress.

This data never leaves your device and is not visible to the developer. You can erase the saved numbers with the "सेव किए इनपुट मिटाओ" button on the home screen, or by clearing the App's data in your phone settings.

Internet use

The calculators and charts work fully offline. When your phone is online, the App may:

  • check the website that hosts the App (GitHub Pages) for a newer version of its own pages;
  • download a list of extra learning-material links from a Google Sheets document;
  • open links that you tap (for example a video or a document).

As with any website, the servers of these services (such as GitHub and Google) may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when the request is made. The developer does not receive or store this information.

Share / Copy button

When you tap "Share / Copy", the result text on your screen is handed to your phone's share menu or clipboard. The App does not send it anywhere by itself. What happens next depends on the app you choose to share with.

Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages, and the optional list of extra material is stored in a Google Sheets document, as described above.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults: tradespeople, students, and professionals in the pipe and structural fabrication field. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Disclaimer

The App is a learning and reference aid. Always verify calculations, dimensions, and standards against your project drawings, specifications, and the applicable codes before fabrication.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this same page with an updated date above.

Contact

For any question about this Privacy Policy, write to the developer at siddiqiimteyaz@gmail.com, or use the Connect section of the App.
