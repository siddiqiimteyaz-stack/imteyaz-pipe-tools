Privacy Policy — IMTEYAZ SKILL HUB

Last updated: August 2026

IMTEYAZ SKILL HUB ("the App") is a pipe & structural fabrication training and calculator app, developed and maintained by an individual developer. This page explains what data the App does and does not collect.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server. All calculators work entirely on your device. The App does not require an internet connection, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not access your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Local Storage

The App may save a small amount of data (such as an unlocked-content preference) directly on your own device using your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not visible to the developer.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children. The content is intended for tradespeople, students, and professionals in the pipe and structural fabrication field.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this same page with an updated date above.

Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the app's Connect section.
